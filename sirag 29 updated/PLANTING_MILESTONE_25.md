Milestone 25: visible gesture-driven planting activity overlay integrated with existing WebGL oasis. Five stages: dig with visible shovel and hole, drag seed into hole, cover sand, water with vessel and darkened soil, brush growing sprout. All stages triggered from completed habit; existing persistence retained. Rendering uses lightweight 2D close-up interaction layered over 3D oasis; not physically simulated 3D soil or photorealistic assets.
