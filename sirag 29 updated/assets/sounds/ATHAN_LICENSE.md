# Adhan recording

- Source: Wikimedia Commons, "File:Beautiful adhan.ogg" by Adam-synagda (own work, 2022-04-29)
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_adhan.ogg
- License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). No attribution required; credited here anyway.
- `athan.wav`: the first two takbir phrases (29.5 s, mono), used as the notification sound.
  iOS plays notification sounds only if they are shorter than 30 seconds.
- `athan_full.mp3`: the full adhan (2 min 33 s), played inside the app when it is open at prayer time.
- Please have someone listen to both files before release.
