# Original notification tones

`wudu_drop.wav`, `wudu_soft.wav`, `prayer_bell.wav`, `prayer_soft.wav` and `gentle.wav` are original non-vocal synthesized cues created for Siraj. They contain no sampled recording or third-party audio. Their reproducible source is `scripts/build-notification-tones.py` (Python standard library only). Format: 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV; all shorter than 3 seconds.

These original cues are dedicated under CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . The separate adhan recording retains its source/license notes in ATHAN_LICENSE.md.
