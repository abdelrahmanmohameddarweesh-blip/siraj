SHORT DESCRIPTION

FULL DESCRIPTION — DRAFT FOR OWNER REVIEW
Build a gentle daily routine with Siraj.

Keep your Quran, prayer, Athkar and memorization habits together in a calm Arabic interface. Choose Night or Dawn appearance and let your oasis reflect the steps you record.

• Prayer times calculated for your selected city and method, with configurable local reminders and personal check-ins.
• Quran reading and listening, with confirmed-page and playback tracking.
• Morning and evening Athkar sessions.
• Memorization and review tools.
• Personal habit goals and detailed activity reports.
• A growing oasis with seeds, water and plants earned from recorded habits.

Reading, prayer and habit completion are personal records, not an independent verification of worship. Notification delivery depends on device permissions, Focus/battery settings and operating-system behavior. Some Quran text and audio sources require an internet connection.

Intended for ages 13 and older. Final listing and privacy declarations require publisher review.
