# Living Desert — Integrated Habit World

- Habit Garden tab now opens the playable Sand World directly. No duplicate checklist, launcher card, or separate navigation required.
- Habit selection, daily completion, persistent plant stages, camera movement, and watering remain in the same full-screen experience.
- Added contextual touch-action orb during care, stage-specific gestures, visual care-progress ring, and cinematic return to exploration.
- **Visual fidelity:** This remains a stylized 2.5D canvas prototype, not a photorealistic 3D game.
- **Daily progression:** Each habit advances once per local calendar day; subsequent actions are available on subsequent days.
- **Testing:** Validate on iPhone with Expo Go; actual hardware has not been tested in this environment.
