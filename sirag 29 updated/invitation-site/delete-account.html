Delete account and data

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Siraj's profile menu on the homepage.
  2. Select الحساب والخصوصية وحذف البيانات (Account and privacy).
  3. Read the deletion summary. If your account is email-linked, enter its password in the app to confirm your identity.
  4. Confirm حذف الحساب والبيانات. Keep an internet connection until completion.

This deletes your circle memberships/shared records, private profile, your submitted reports, authentication account and saved records/reminders on that device. Circle ownership can transfer to another member. Your private journals on other phones are not automatically erased; clear those devices separately. If interrupted, the app keeps a resumable deletion state and does not claim completion.

Request deletion without reinstalling

Email the Siraj publisher using the button below. Identify the account with its registered email, or provide its account ID from Siraj's account screen if you use an anonymous account. If available, provide enough circle context to help identify the record. We verify ownership before deleting data; another person's account cannot be deleted solely because someone submits its name.

Request deletion by email

You may also copy the address: abdelrahman.mohamed.darweesh@gmail.com. Opening this page or your email app does not itself submit or complete deletion. Do not send passwords, payment information or private keys.

Independent reports submitted by others and Google-managed advertising/security records can be retained for their separate purposes under the privacy policy.

طلب حذف الحساب والبيانات

يمكنك الحذف من داخل التطبيق: قائمة الملف الشخصي ← الحساب والخصوصية ← حذف الحساب والبيانات. إذا لم تستطع الوصول إلى التطبيق، أرسل طلبًا إلى البريد أعلاه، واذكر البريد المرتبط أو رقم الحساب إن كان متاحًا. نتحقّق من الملكية قبل التنفيذ. لا ترسل كلمة المرور. فتح هذه الصفحة وحده لا يحذف الحساب.
