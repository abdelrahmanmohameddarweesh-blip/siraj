Who to contact

This policy covers the Siraj Android app (com.sirag.livingdesert) and its circle invitation/support pages. The publisher shown on Siraj's Google Play listing operates the app. Public support and privacy contact: abdelrahman.mohamed.darweesh@gmail.com.

Profile and sign-in

Siraj saves your chosen name and gender on your device and may synchronize them to a private Firebase profile. Firebase Anonymous Authentication supplies a persistent installation identity. Email/password linking is optional for account recovery; Firebase handles those credentials. Your email, gender and exact activity records are not displayed in a public leaderboard. Circle name/status sharing is controlled separately.

Worship records and circles

Prayer check-ins, Quran reading/listening, Athkar, memorization and habit journals are primarily stored on your device. If you join a circle, the selected habit's daily quantity, completion status and percentage are synchronized to member-private Firestore records. The circle may share your permitted name or alias, completion/percentage and aggregate counts according to its creator's settings and your personal choices. Religious activity can reveal sensitive personal interests or beliefs; sharing a circle is optional, and you may hide your name/status or leave.

Circle metadata is unlisted. A person who knows a random circle ID may view its basic name/settings and anonymous membership aliases, but membership is required to read protected progress. Anyone holding a current invitation code can join until the cap or journey expiry is reached. Share codes only with intended companions. Moderation reports include the reporting account ID and the text you submit; project administrators can review them.

Location, media and notifications

Device location is optional for calculating prayer times. Manual city selection is available. Siraj's prayer calculations use the selected coordinates locally; the app does not intentionally attach your coordinates to ad requests. Local notification settings and reminder schedules are kept on the phone. Quran audio and other external media services receive normal network information such as IP address when accessed. No account is required on an external media service merely to use Siraj's local trackers.

Optional rewarded advertising

Supported native builds may offer explicitly optional rewarded ads for garden seeds or water. Watching an ad does not complete worship records or increase prayer credit. The Google Mobile Ads and consent SDKs may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices under Google's policies. Siraj requests non-personalized ads and a general-audience content rating; this does not mean no data is processed. We do not send your name, gender, Firebase UID or worship journal as ad-targeting fields. Ad requests follow the applicable Google consent flow. Where provided, use the offer sheet's ad privacy options to change choices. Ads are not required to access worship features.

Storage, retention and deletion

Device records remain until you remove them or erase the app's data. Circle and private profile records are retained to provide the service until you leave/delete them or request deletion. In the app, open the profile menu → Account and privacy → Delete account and data. The flow removes your circle memberships/shared records, private profile, reports submitted by you, authentication account and this device's saved records/reminders. It may transfer circle ownership to a remaining member. Internet access and recent authentication may be required. An interrupted deletion is kept pending and can be resumed; the app does not report success before it completes.

Deleting an account does not automatically erase journals already stored on other phones; clear those devices separately. Reports submitted independently by other people and Google-managed ad/security records may remain subject to their own legitimate purposes and retention rules. If you cannot access the app, use the web deletion-request instructions. We verify account ownership before acting. Do not email passwords or private keys.

Security and choices

Firebase network access uses its secure SDK connections. Access rules restrict private records to their owner and shared circle data to permitted members/settings. No system can guarantee absolute security. You may decline optional location, notifications and rewarded ads, avoid circles, use aliases, or delete your account. Siraj does not sell your worship journal. Service providers process data as described above; Google advertising may involve sharing of technical information under its applicable terms.

Age eligibility

Siraj is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not intended for children under 13. This stated audience is not an age-verification system. If you believe a child under 13 has supplied personal data, contact support to request review and deletion. Teenagers may have additional privacy rights or require a parent or guardian's involvement under applicable local law. Live ads remain disabled in the initial production configuration.
